# Shina Halavi Voice Examples

Use these examples to illustrate how `shinas-voice` should transform prose while
remaining style-only.

## Example 1: Paragraph-Level Smoothing

### Neutral draft

The client has had trouble paying attention for a long time. She loses focus
easily, especially when reading or when the material is not interesting. This
has made work and studying harder for her.

### Voice-applied draft

CLIENT endorsed long-standing challenges with attention and concentration,
particularly when reading or completing material that she does not find
interesting. Specifically, she described becoming easily distracted and losing
focus during these tasks, which has negatively impacted her work and studying.

### What changed

- casual phrasing shifted to clinician-preferred attribution verbs
- sentence cadence moved from short note-style statements to a topic-plus-detail structure
- functional impact was stated in the corpus-preferred register without adding new facts

## Example 2: Attribution Refinement

### Neutral draft

Her mother said she was anxious in social situations as a child and had trouble
joining other kids.

### Voice-applied draft

Per mother report, CLIENT demonstrated anxiety in social situations during
childhood and experienced difficulty joining peer interactions.

### What changed

- attribution was made explicit in the clinician’s preferred format
- vocabulary shifted from casual to polished clinical language
- meaning stayed the same

## Example 3: Section Overlay Use

### Neutral draft

CLIENT is a 34-year-old woman who came for testing because she has had ongoing
problems with concentration, anxiety, and organization. The goal is to clarify
what is going on and help with treatment.

### Voice-applied draft

CLIENT is a 34-year-old, right-handed female who is seeking an outpatient
neuropsychological assessment in the context of longstanding difficulties with
attention, concentration, organization, and anxiety. The purpose of this
evaluation is to determine strengths and weaknesses and to assist with
diagnostic clarification and treatment planning and care.

### What changed

- reason-for-referral template was applied
- concern cluster was reframed with `in the context of ...`
- standardized purpose sentence was used

## Example Rule

Examples should demonstrate:

- diction change without factual drift
- cadence change without meaning change
- attribution preservation
- uncertainty preservation
