# Style Contract

This artifact governs the **voice-generation** pass. It may influence tone,
ordering, and sentence shape, but it may not introduce new factual content.

Use this together with `reference/report-polish-guide.md`. If the guide and the
case-specific contract differ, this case-specific contract wins.

## Target Genre

- `Polished outpatient neuropsychological history-report prose`

## Voice Rules

- Write in clinician-native narrative prose, not workflow or audit prose.
- Prefer `CLIENT described...`, `CLIENT endorsed...`, `mother reported...`, and `records indicate...`.
- Keep the prose concrete and developmentally anchored.
- Preserve the contrast between strong objective academic performance and substantial subjective effort/inefficiency.
- Keep diagnostic restraint: describe patterns clearly without converting referral or differential language into confirmed diagnosis.

## Paragraph Construction Rules

Each substantive paragraph should usually do most of the following:

1. open with the clinical pattern
2. locate it developmentally or chronologically
3. include 1-3 concrete examples
4. state the current functional impact
5. end with a bounded interpretive synthesis when justified

## Tone Rules

- Sound clinically fluent and concise rather than exhaustive.
- Qualify naturally when records are thin, especially in family and occupational history.
- Do not pad sparse sections with generic filler.
- Use concrete examples sparingly but meaningfully; each example should illustrate the pattern or its impact.
- Keep likely readers in mind and include only the depth they need to understand pattern, impact, chronology, and planning implications.
- Prefer `rigid patterns of behavior` or `rigid adherence to routines` over centering quoted shorthand.
- Summarize family history at the immediate/extended-family level unless relative-specific detail materially changes interpretation.
- Omit incidental medical anecdotes without sequelae unless they affect interpretation.

## Exemplar Policy

- No case-specific style exemplar was directly inspected for this run.
- The bundled polish guide may be used only for generic genre conventions around paragraph density, ordering, and clinician-native phrasing.
- No external exemplar may contribute facts, dates, examples, diagnoses, or conclusions.

## Rewrite Guardrail

- Every substantive sentence in the polished draft must be traceable to one or more approved claims or approved examples in `03-derived/section-claim-bank.md`.
